Module 1: Introduction to Biostatistics

Biostatistics for Clinical Medicine

🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, you should be able to:

  • Define biostatistics and explain its importance in medicine.
  • Describe how statistics are used in clinical research.
  • Distinguish between descriptive and inferential statistics.
  • Identify common types of data used in health research.

📖 Introduction

Biostatistics is the application of statistical methods to biological, medical and health-related data.

In clinical medicine, biostatistics helps healthcare professionals understand patient data, evaluate treatments, interpret research findings and make evidence-based decisions.

💡 Clinical Example

Suppose a hospital wants to determine whether a new treatment reduces the recovery time of patients. Researchers could collect information about patients' recovery times and use statistical methods to compare patients receiving the new treatment with those receiving standard care.

Biostatistics provides the tools needed to determine whether the observed difference is likely to represent a real effect.
