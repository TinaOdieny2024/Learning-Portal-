Welcome to your online learning portal. Explore course modules, learning resources, assignments and assessments as you progress through the 12-week Biostatistics course.

This course is designed to help you move beyond simply calculating statistics. You will learn how to understand clinical data, select appropriate statistical methods, interpret results and use statistical evidence to support sound clinical and research decisions.

Help & Support

How do I open a module?

Go to Course Modules and select Open Module on any module that has been released.

Why is a module locked?

A locked module has not yet reached its scheduled release date and time. It will become available automatically when the release time is reached.

How is my progress recorded?

When you mark a module as completed, the portal records your progress in your browser on your current device.
