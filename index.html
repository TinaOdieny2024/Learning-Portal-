Welcome to the Course

This portal provides a structured learning pathway through the major concepts and practical applications of biostatistics in Clinical Medicine. The course moves from understanding clinical data and describing datasets to probability, sampling, estimation, hypothesis testing, group comparisons, correlation and regression, clinical epidemiology, and statistical interpretation in clinical research.

Each module contains a brief introduction, learning content, a module PDF, and an end-of-module quiz. Students should log in to access their learning area and follow the module release schedule.

Start here: Click Student Login if you are a student, or Instructor Portal if you are the course instructor.

Practical Learning

Practical activities progressively develop skills in data entry and cleaning, descriptive analysis, probability and sampling, confidence intervals, hypothesis testing, group comparisons, correlation and regression, diagnostic statistics, and complete clinical data analysis.

Recommended software: Microsoft Excel and Jamovi (or SPSS).
